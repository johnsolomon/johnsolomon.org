Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy describes how John Solomon LLC ("we") handles information in our apps and on this website.

What we collect

  • Account sign-in. If you create an account, we store the sign-in identifier you choose (such as an email address or Sign in with Apple identifier) so you can access your account.
  • Study progress. We store your progress, such as answered questions, scores and saved items, so it is kept with your account and available across your devices.

We do not collect health information. We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or tracking tools.

How we use it

We use this information only to provide the app's features, keep your progress, and respond to support requests. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except service providers that host our systems on our behalf, or where required by law.

Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing help@johnsolomon.org; we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Our apps are intended for adults preparing for professional exams and are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the current version.
